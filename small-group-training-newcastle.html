WHO IS THIS FOR?

Private small-group training suits people who like training with others, want real coaching rather than unsupervised gym access, and value a structured approach to improving strength and fitness.

Yes. HARD GYM works with a range of ages and experience levels and tailors coaching so participants can train at an appropriate level.
