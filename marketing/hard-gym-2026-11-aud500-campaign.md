## Conversion tracking — required before enabling spend
**Current website audit (18 Nov 2026):** No visible GA4 measurement ID, Google Tag Manager container, Google Ads conversion tag or Meta Pixel in the checked homepage and consultation page. The consultation form submits to `formsubmit.co/info@hardgym.com.au` with a spam/CAPTCHA step and **no** `_next` thank-you redirect. Simply clicking Submit **must not** count as a successful lead. Website signup "JOIN THE CREW" must be measured separately from the free-consultation form.

**Implement only after IDs and consent decisions are confirmed:**
1. Create/confirm **GA4 web data stream**, **GTM container**, **Google Ads conversion action** and **Meta Pixel/dataset**. Use only real IDs from the owner; do not invent IDs.
2. Provide a clear privacy notice and appropriate **consent mechanism** before activating marketing pixels. Explain third-party trackers and how visitors can opt out. Do not send form fields, names, emails, goals or phone numbers as ordinary analytics event parameters. Review Australia's OAIC tracking-pixel guidance.
3. For an actual consultation submission, use FormSubmit's documented `_next` redirect to a dedicated, non-indexed **consultation thank-you page**, but only after testing CAPTCHA, form delivery and iframe behaviour on mobile/desktop. Tag the successful destination with GA4 `generate_lead` and Google Ads "Lead" conversion; configure Meta `Lead` to fire once with consent. Do not count clicks or field entry as completed enquiries. Test against duplicate reloads and direct URL visits; server confirmation is preferable if technically possible.
4. Track diagnostic events **`consultation_cta_click`**, **`consultation_form_start`** and **`consultation_form_submit_attempt`** separately from success. Filter spam/invalid leads in manual records.
5. Mark **qualified consultation enquiry** as the primary performance metric, and record **consultation booked**, **consultation attended**, and **new paying client** with source/campaign fields in a private sheet or CRM. Never upload personal contact details to advertising tools without appropriate consent and configuration.
6. Use UTM tags and Google's auto-tagging for attribution. Keep the `gclid` intact as appropriate; confirm cross-domain behaviour around FormSubmit redirects and iframes.
7. Before launch, test from an incognito browser: ad URL/UTM, form validation, successful submit, actual email receipt, consent gating, GA4 DebugView, Ads diagnostic, Pixel Test Events, unique conversion once, and performance on iPhone/iPad/desktop.

## 30-day operating plan (after explicit approval)
- **Before day 1:** Audit working pages and contact form; connect verified ad accounts; deploy and test tracking/consent; upload approved creatives; create Google and Meta campaigns in **PAUSED** state; review final budgets/tax treatment. Require explicit owner approval to enable.
- **Days 1–7:** Limit to local search and one Meta audience. Review actual search terms and geographic relevance, reject bot/spam, check form submission reliability. Avoid changing many variables simultaneously.
- **Days 8–14:** Pause low-quality keywords or placements. Measure cost per real enquiry and contact response time. Improve the destination page if users click but do not submit.
- **Days 15–21:** Shift a limited amount of remaining spend toward the best source **only after** observing real consultations, not raw clicks. Keep total approved media cap.
- **Days 22–30:** Decide whether to continue, stop, or scale based on qualified cost per lead, consultations attended and actual clients signed.

**Stop/hold rules:** Do not enable ads before tracking is verified. Pause immediately if the form breaks, ads attract out-of-area contacts, or consent/tracking is misconfigured. Review after roughly A$100 of spend without a verified lead; diagnose rather than automatically increasing budget.

## Evidence and constraints
- Google Ads permits local radius targeting and recommends using the presence option when you want local residents rather than people simply interested in an area.
- Google Ads budgets can vary by day (up to 2× daily budget), with monthly limits for most campaigns of 30.4×.
- Responsive search ads allow up to 15 headlines (30 chars each) and 4 descriptions (90 chars each).
- FormSubmit supports an `_next` redirect after an HTML submission. The redirect must be tested to verify correct conversion behaviour.
- Australian OAIC advises that third-party tracking pixels may carry privacy obligations; consent and transparency should be assessed before installation.
